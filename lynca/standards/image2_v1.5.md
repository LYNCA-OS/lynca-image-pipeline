# LYNCA Image2 Preview Standard v1.5

## Core Rule
One input image → one output image

---

## Composition
- Single card only
- Full slab/holder must be visible
- No cropping edges
- Centered

---

## Background
- White or light gray only
- Clean, no clutter

---

## Orientation
- Keep original orientation
- Do NOT rotate or fix reversed text

---

## Preservation
- Keep autograph
- Keep patch texture
- Keep serial numbers
- Keep labels
- Keep gradients

---

## Identity Lock
- Do NOT alter player face or body
- No reshaping

---

## Processing
Allowed:
- Mild perspective fix
- Centering
- Light exposure adjust

Forbidden:
- Over sharpening
- Heavy contrast
- HDR look
- Repainting details
- Fixing text readability

---

## Principle
Stable > Perfect
Realistic > Clean
